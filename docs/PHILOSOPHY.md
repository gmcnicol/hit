# HIT philosophy

## Intention over implementation

HIT captures what the composer wants the listener to experience before prescribing how that experience must be produced.

The user should be able to ask for:

- more anticipation;
- less density;
- preserve intimacy;
- introduce sparkle later;
- make the ending wider rather than louder;
- increase motion without adding weight;
- delay the payoff.

The Composition Mapper responds with structural and musical suggestions. It does not begin with EQ, compression, saturation, LUFS or mastering instructions.

> The Composition Mapper captures intention, not implementation.

## The musician provides the vocabulary

HIT does not invent the riff, hook or chord passage. The musician records the source material.

An idea may be one recorded thing. It does not require pre-recorded doubles, harmonies, counterlines or texture layers.

When the composition would benefit from something that does not yet exist, HIT suggests it clearly as an unrealised layer intention. It never pretends missing audio exists.

> The musician creates the vocabulary. HIT helps form the language.

## Compose before producing

A weak arrangement should not need to be rescued with permanent compression, saturation, corrective EQ or indiscriminate layering.

HIT aims to create compositions with natural:

- contrast;
- space;
- development;
- punch;
- fullness;
- detail;
- novelty;
- restraint;
- release.

The mix should refine the composition, not rescue it.

## Evolution over duplication

Repeated material may return with a different pickup, alternative, turnaround, overlap, duration, context or layer suggestion.

The user should never feel that they are merely editing copies. They are shaping:

- the idea;
- its phrase components;
- its occurrences;
- its evolution;
- the intention of each passage.

## Long-form composition is first-class

HIT must support long sections, irregular lengths, fixed openings, loopable middles, fixed endings, sustained tails, multi-minute drones, delayed returns, gradual replacement and false endings.

It must never assume that music is a collection of interchangeable four-bar loops.

> An idea is a musical object with its own grammar.

## Suggest, do not dictate

The default relationship is composer and assistant.

HIT proposes, explains and revises. The musician listens and decides.

Manual editing remains possible, but the primary interaction is direction:

- more of this;
- less of that;
- slower build;
- later climax;
- more overlap;
- make the middle breathe.

## Interesting over loud

HIT should favour contrast over permanent intensity, selective width over constant width, and deliberate withdrawal over endless accumulation.

A high-energy passage does not mean every possible layer is active.

## Evidence without servitude

Airwindows Meter and internal analysis are evidence about the consequences of compositional choices. They are not artistic authorities.

Scores do not replace listening. HIT uses them to learn which compositional decisions tend to create pleasing, balanced and aurally interesting results.

## Non-destructive by default

Source recordings are immutable to the compiler.

Generated demos are disposable, reproducible outputs. The composition map and source material remain the truth.

## The destination is art

The campaign does not end with a software demonstration.

It ends with a finished musical work, a deliberate Afterimage visual arrangement and a publishable YouTube video.
